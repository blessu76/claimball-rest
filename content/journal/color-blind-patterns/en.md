Claimball is a game about two colors on a grid, so color alone is a weak signal for some players. This post covers two small changes from the test builds. They are not released to the public yet.

## Color-blind patterns

A new setting, Color-blind patterns, puts dots on your tiles and stripes on the opponent's. You can read the board by pattern even if the two colors look alike. It arrived in build 5.

## Easier small text

In build 9 we improved text contrast a little in both the Classic and Notebook looks, so small text is easier to read.

## Try it yourself

You can turn the pattern setting on and off at any time, so it is easy to compare.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet. Every build is listed on the [release notes](/releases/) page.
