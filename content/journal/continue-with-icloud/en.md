Build 7, a TestFlight internal test build, lets you pick up where you left off on another device. It is not released to the public yet.

## What follows your iCloud account

On iPhone and iPad, your progress, stars, achievements, the free-trial state and your accessibility settings follow your iCloud account. It works quietly in the background. If iCloud is off or offline, the game simply keeps its own copy on the device.

## What stays on the device

Some things are deliberately not shared through iCloud: the Full Unlock purchase (the App Store keeps track of that), the Daily Challenge attempt, and vibration and sound settings.

## Careful with purchases

If a purchase is refunded or taken back through Family Sharing, the game locks again. It is careful not to lock by mistake when you are signed out or offline.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
