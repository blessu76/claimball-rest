Since build 14, a TestFlight internal test build, Claimball opens your very first match with a short How to play guide. It is not released to the public yet.

## Three cards, then you play

The guide is three cards, so you can read it in a few seconds and get into the match. Claimball is a 90-second duel on a 6 by 10 notebook grid, and the basics fit on a card: the tile where the ball bounces becomes the land of the player who hit it, and hitting with the center of your paddle gives a smash that claims 3 tiles at once.

## Why we added it

A game you play in short sessions has to explain itself quickly. We would rather you spend your first minute playing than hunting for the rules.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
