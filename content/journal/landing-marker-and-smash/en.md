Build 1, the first TestFlight internal test build, already had the core of Claimball. It is not released to the public yet.

## A 90-second match on a grid

A match lasts about 90 seconds on a grid of tiles. Hit the ball to paint tiles your color. Be first to claim 30 tiles, or hold more tiles when time runs out.

## The landing marker

A marker shows where the ball will land, so you can get your paddle there in time instead of guessing.

## The smash

Keep the rally going to fill the smash gauge. Then hit the ball with the center of your paddle for a smash, which claims 3 tiles at once.

## Ties and the last ball

A tie goes to sudden death, where the next point wins. If a ball is still in the air at 90 seconds, the match waits for it to land before deciding.

## Controls and pausing

You can play with drag, keyboard or touch. The game pauses automatically when you leave the app, and since build 2 a pause button during a match (Esc or P on a keyboard) offers Resume, Restart and Quit.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
