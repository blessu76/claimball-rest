Build 15, a TestFlight internal test build, adds two things you can see and hear. It is not released to the public yet.

## My Stats

The new My Stats screen shows your wins, draws and losses, your record against each opponent, and your longest rally. These records are kept only on your device.

## Background music

Claimball now has three original tracks: one for the menu, one for matches, and a faster fever tempo. You can turn the music on or off with a toggle in Settings.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
