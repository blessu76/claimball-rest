Build 9, a TestFlight internal test build, gave the game a second look. It is not released to the public yet.

## Notebook

Notebook is a paper-and-grid-notebook style that matches this website, which suits a game played on a notebook grid.

## How to switch

Pick Classic or Notebook in Settings, at the bottom of the settings card. You can switch at any time, even in the middle of a session, and nothing restarts. Classic stays the default, so the game looks the same until you change it.

## Per device

Your theme choice is saved on each device and is not shared through iCloud, so a phone and a tablet can use different looks.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet. Every build is listed on the [release notes](/releases/) page.
