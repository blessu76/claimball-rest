**In short:** the Claimball app collects no personal information and contains no analytics. This website collects basic visit statistics through Google Analytics, remembers your language in your own browser, and asks for no name, email or account. There are no ads and we do not sell data.

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE}} · Operator: {{OPERATOR}}

## 1. Information we collect

The Claimball app ("the App") is a mobile game operated by an individual developer, {{OPERATOR}}. The App has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect or send to any server any personal information such as your name, email, contact details, location or device identifiers. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. This applies to the App only; the website is described in section 2.

## 2. The website

### Visit statistics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 on claimball.ipe.rest to see which pages are read. It records page views, the page you came from, your browser and device type, an approximate location (country or city) and clicks on links that leave our site (the link address only). It sets cookies in your browser (such as `_ga`, which can last up to two years) to tell visits apart. We do not ask for your name or email in analytics, and we cannot identify you from it. Google processes this data on our behalf, which can include servers outside Korea. We use it only to understand what is read and to improve the site; we do not use it for profiling and we do not sell it.

### Your choices

Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's [Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The site works the same without them.

### Other

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so ordinary web request information (such as your IP address) may be sent to Google. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub's server-log policies apply. If you pick a language, your browser may keep that choice on your own device only; it is never sent to us.

## 3. Third parties

The App collects no personal information. For the website, Google (analytics and fonts) and GitHub (hosting) process data as described above; we do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires. The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play; information related to downloads is handled by each store under its own privacy policy.
