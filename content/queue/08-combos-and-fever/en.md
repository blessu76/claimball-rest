Build 5, a TestFlight internal test build, gave a Claimball match more of a rhythm. It is not released to the public yet.

## Combo chains

Keep the rally going and the game answers you. The sound and the haptic buzz step up at 3, 5, 8 and 12 hits in a row.

## Fever

In the last 20 seconds a Fever mode switches on: a warmer screen tone, bouncing tiles and a quick beep. The rules stay exactly the same, so Fever changes how the end of a match feels, not how it is decided.

## Stars and achievements

Each match earns 1 to 3 stars. Your best stars show on each rival's card, and a list of achievements keeps track of the rest.

## Rivals that react

The six AI rivals now react with faces and little movements when they enter, score, get scored on, are surprised, win or lose. Beat one to unlock the next, and your progress is saved.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet. Every build is listed on the [release notes](/releases/) page.
