Builds 3 and 4, TestFlight internal test builds, changed how Claimball looks, feels and sounds. They are not released to the public yet.

## A softer look

Build 3 brought a toy-like look: rounded, glossy tiles, a new ball and paddle, and a fresh court. The scoreboard and overlays match. On small phones the board now keeps a little margin on both sides and room for its shadow.

## A little vibration

Your paddle hit now gives a small vibration, lighter for a normal hit and firmer for a smash. You can turn it off in Settings.

## A new opening

Build 4 added an intro screen with a short pop sound, a redesigned menu and a redesigned results screen. The score chips on the match screen were tidied up. The intro sound is made for the game.

## Sound On/Off

A new Sound On/Off setting is on by default, so you can play quietly whenever you like.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
