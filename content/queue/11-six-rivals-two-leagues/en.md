Build 5, a TestFlight internal test build, gave Claimball someone to play against. It is not released to the public yet.

## Six rivals, two leagues

There are six AI rivals, split into two leagues. Each one plays a little differently, from a gentle first opponent to a sharp last one.

## Pick your opponent

You choose who to face. Beat a rival to unlock the next, and your progress is saved. Each rival's card shows your best stars against them.

## Checked with simulated matches

To keep the ladder fair, we run many simulated matches between a plain test bot and each rival and compare how often the rival wins. One rival turned out to be a much bigger jump than the others, so we eased it. The same simulations also confirm that the landing marker matches where the ball really lands.

## Still in tuning

These numbers come from bots, not from people. How the ladder feels in real hands is something we are still learning from testers.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
