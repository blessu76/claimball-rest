Claimball is not only about claiming tiles. It is also about what you lose when you miss. These rules are in the TestFlight internal test builds, which are not released to the public yet.

## A miss costs tiles

When a ball gets past you, you lose 2 tiles. If you are leading by 10 or more tiles when it happens, you lose 3 instead.

## Why a bigger penalty when ahead

A large lead can make the rest of a match feel decided. The extra tile makes a big lead a little less comfortable, so the player who is behind always has a way back.

## Two ways to win

Be first to claim 30 tiles, or hold more tiles when the 90 seconds end. A tie goes to sudden death, where the next point wins. A ball still in the air at 90 seconds is allowed to land before the match is decided.

## What it adds up to

Because tiles can be lost as well as won, a match swings back and forth instead of only growing one way. That is why even a one-sided start can still turn around.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
