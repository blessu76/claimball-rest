Build 6, a TestFlight internal test build, added how Claimball is planned to be paid for. It is not released to the public yet, and the price has not been announced.

## Try first

Claimball is free to try. You can play and get a taste of the rivals before you decide anything.

## One purchase

A single purchase unlocks everything. There are no ads and no subscription. The amount is shown by the store in your own currency when it is available.

## Calm, not pushy

Locked rival cards simply look locked, and the purchase sheet is shown only at a few natural moments, such as after a match you won, at most once per launch. There is no pressure wording.

## Restore and waiting

If you bought before, a Restore button in Settings brings the unlock back. If a purchase is waiting for approval, for example under Ask to Buy, the game waits instead of guessing.

## If a purchase is refunded

A refund removes the unlock, but only after the game has seen the store say so on two separate launches, so a temporary sign-in glitch does not lock you out by mistake.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
