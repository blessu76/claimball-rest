Build 22, a TestFlight internal test build, is about feel. It is not released to the public yet.

## A shake when you miss

When you lose a ball, the screen gives a light shake and a vibration, so a miss registers right away.

## A sparkle when the smash is ready

When the smash gauge fills, your paddle sparkles. You can see at a glance that the next center hit will be a smash.

## Different endings

When a match ends, winning and losing each get their own vibration.

## Rally Party

The Daily Challenge has a new twist called Rally Party. The ball is slower and speeds up gently, so long rallies are easier to keep going. We checked it with simulated matches, and the landing marker still matched every time.

## Still to check

How strong the vibration feels and whether Rally Party is really fun can only be judged on a real phone, and that is still being tested. Vibration can be turned off in Settings.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
