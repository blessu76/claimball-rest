Build 12, a TestFlight internal test build, added a bit of identity and bragging rights. It is not released to the public yet.

## A nickname

You can set your own nickname from the menu. It is just a name to show on your ranking line.

## A score on every result

The results screen now has a score line, so a match ends with a number as well as a win or a loss.

## A ranking screen

The menu has a ranking screen inside the app, so you can see how your scores stack up.

## Fair by design

The score is worked out from how the match went, and your first Daily Challenge attempt of the day is the one that counts, which fits the one-try-a-day rule.

## Still being tested

We are still checking how these screens behave on real phones.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.
