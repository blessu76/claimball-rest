Puck Rush is the bonus stage of Claimball. You drag a paddle to move and bat the ball. Cells the ball crosses take the color of the last hitter, and a goal paints the area in front of it. When time runs out, the side with the most cells wins.

## Pick a difficulty

The start card shows Easy, Normal and Hard. The last level you chose is saved on the device, so it is ready the next time you play.

## Three different opponents

Hard does not just move faster. It reads where the ball will arrive after bouncing off the walls, and it moves out from its half to meet the ball early. Normal waits near the goal line and meets the ball later.

## Checked before the build

We check the levels by letting the computer play itself in many matches. How the levels feel on a real phone is still being checked.

## Still being tested

Puck Rush is still in testing and the difficulty choice is not yet final.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.

## FAQ

### How do I choose a difficulty?
On the Puck Rush start card, pick Easy, Normal or Hard. Your last choice is remembered on the device.

### Is Hard just a faster Normal?
No. Hard reads where the ball will arrive and moves out to meet it, while Normal waits near the goal line.

### Is Puck Rush released yet?
Not yet. It is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android, and there is no release date yet.
