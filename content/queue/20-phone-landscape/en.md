Claimball used to stay upright on a phone. Now a phone can also hold the game sideways, and the screen turns with you.

## What changes in landscape

On a short phone screen held sideways, the board fills the height of the screen. A compact panel sits on the left and right, so the play area stays as large as possible. The menu also uses two columns in landscape.

## Rotating during a match

The board is kept when you turn the phone, so the match does not restart. If you rotate while a match is running, the game pauses. Press Resume to carry on.

## Screen orientation in Settings

Settings has a Screen orientation row with three choices:

- Auto-rotate: follows the way you hold the phone.
- Portrait: keeps the screen upright.
- Landscape: keeps the screen sideways.

The choice is saved on the device, so it stays the same the next time you open the app.

## Still being tested

Landscape on a phone is still in testing. The layout is checked at several phone and tablet sizes, but it is not final yet.

## What is next

Claimball is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android. There is no release date yet.

## FAQ

### Does the game keep going when I turn my phone?
The board stays as it is when you rotate. If a match is running, it pauses, so press Resume to continue.

### Can I lock the screen to one direction?
Yes. In Settings, choose Portrait or Landscape instead of Auto-rotate.

### Is landscape released yet?
Not yet. It is still in testing and coming soon to iOS and Android, and there is no release date yet.
