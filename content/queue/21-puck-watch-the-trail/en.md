In Puck Rush, the tiles the puck crosses take the color of the side that last hit it. A return can change the board even when it never reaches the goal.

## Try it in a match

After one return, follow the puck for a moment and notice which strip of tiles changed. Try comparing a return through the middle with one that travels near a wall.

## FAQ

### Do I need a goal to paint tiles?

No. The puck paints the tiles it crosses.

### What should I watch after a return?

Look at the strip behind the puck and see how much of the board it crossed.
