In Puck Rush, the cells the ball crosses take the color of the side that last hit it. A return can change the board even when it never reaches the goal.

## Try it in a match

After one return, follow the ball for a moment and notice which strip of cells changed. Try comparing a return through the middle with one that travels near a wall.

## FAQ

### Do I need a goal to paint cells?

No. The ball paints the cells it crosses.

### What should I watch after a return?

Look at the strip behind the ball and see how much of the board it crossed.
