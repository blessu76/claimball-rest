Soccer offers a useful idea for Puck Rush: get between the puck and your goal before you try to win it back. It gives you a place to defend while you see where the puck is heading.

## Try it in a match

When the puck is off to one side, check whether following it would leave the goal open. Try staying goal-side for one exchange and see what arrives in reach.

## FAQ

### Is the soccer comparison about scoring?

It is about where you stand while defending.

### When should I try it?

Try it when the puck moves toward one side and you have room to reposition.
