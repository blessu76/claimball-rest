A return can leave your paddle near one edge of the board. Between shots, move it partway back toward the middle so you have room to go either way.

## Try it in a match

Try it after a wide return. Watch where the next ball heads, then make the last adjustment from your new position.

## FAQ

### Do I have to reach the exact center?

Move back only as far as you have time for.

### Which shot is a good time to practice?

Start after a return that leaves your paddle near an edge.
