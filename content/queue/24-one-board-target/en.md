On the notebook grid, a bounce claims a tile. Pick one tile before the exchange and use it as a reference when the ball lands.

## Try it in a match

After the bounce, notice whether the ball landed to the left or right of the tile you picked. Keep the same target for another exchange before picking a new one.

## FAQ

### Does the game set a target for me?

This is a practice prompt. You choose the reference tile.

### Why keep the same tile for another exchange?

It gives you the same reference when you compare the two landings.
