You can choose which unlocked AI rival to face. Play the same rival again when you want to practice one thing under familiar conditions.

## Try it in a match

Pick a single cue for the second match, such as getting the paddle back into position after a wide return. Afterward, think about when you had time to do it.

## FAQ

### Do I need to move up a league to practice?

You can choose an unlocked rival you already know.

### What should I compare after two matches?

Compare how often you noticed the cue you chose.
