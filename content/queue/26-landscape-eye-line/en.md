In landscape on a phone, the board takes its height from the short side of the screen and has panels alongside it. A quick glance during a quiet moment can help you learn where those panels sit.

## Try it in a match

Keep your eyes on the board during a rally. When play pauses, look to the side and then back to the board. Try portrait on another match if that feels easier to follow.

## FAQ

### When should I look at a side panel?

A pause gives you time to look without losing track of the rally.

### Can I try portrait on the next match?

Yes. You can choose a screen orientation in settings.
