In Puck Rush, the ball can rebound off a wall before it reaches you. On a slower approach, try watching the turn at the wall so you can meet the path on your side.

## Try it in a match

Start with a ball you have time to watch. Compare where you thought it would go with where it goes after the bounce, then adjust your paddle.

## FAQ

### Which ball should I practice with?

Pick a slow approach so you have time to see the bounce.

### What changes after the wall bounce?

The path turns away from the wall. Track the new direction before you finish moving.
