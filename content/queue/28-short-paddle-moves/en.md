A ball that comes close to your paddle can call for a small adjustment. Try a short move so you can see how much input you actually need.

## Try it in a match

Pick one exchange where the ball is already nearby. Make a small correction, watch where it meets the paddle, and try the same motion again when you see a similar ball.

## FAQ

### Do short moves work for every ball?

This drill is for a ball that is already close to the paddle.

### What should I watch at contact?

Notice which part of the paddle the ball meets.
