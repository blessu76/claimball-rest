The colors on the notebook grid show which side owns each claimed tile. When you have a moment between exchanges, look for a small cluster that has changed color.

## Try it in a match

Pick one corner of the board or one row to scan on your next glance. Then look back at the ball so you can follow the next exchange.

## FAQ

### Should I count tiles during a rally?

Use a quick glance when you have a quiet moment.

### Where can I start looking?

Pick a corner or a row so there is a small area to scan.
