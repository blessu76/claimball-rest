In Claimball, a smash hit with the center of the paddle claims three tiles. To get a feel for that position, pay attention to the point of contact when the ball comes straight toward you.

## Try it in a match

Compare that contact with the way you return a ball that makes you reach sideways. Try the center again when you have time to line it up.

## FAQ

### Which contact should I practice first?

Start with a ball that gives you time to line up the paddle center.

### What is the difference to look for?

Notice how much positioning time you have on a straight approach and a wide one.
